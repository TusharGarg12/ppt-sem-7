Row 1 : Research Paper | Published at | Summary | Novelty
Row 2 : 1. A stacking ensemble model for food demand forecasting: A preventative approach to food waste reduction | Cleaner Logistics and Supply Chain (Elsevier) 2025 | Proposes a hybrid machine learning model (combining Random Forest, XGBoost, LSTM, etc.) to dynamically predict demand and optimize daily retail inventory. | Integrates specific "freshness" variables into the algorithm and directly measures the model's practical impact on reducing day-to-day food waste.
Row 3 : 2. Time Series-Based Demand Forecasting: A Comparative Analysis of Holt-Winters, SARIMA, and Prophet Models on Retail Inventory Data | Istanbul Ticaret Üniversitesi Bilimler Dergisi 2025 | Evaluates classical time-series models against a large-scale retail inventory dataset to predict daily product demand and manage stock. | Demonstrates that interpretable, classical models (like Holt-Winters) can actually outperform complex AI in capturing seasonal retail trends, offering a highly practical business solution.
Row 4 : 3. Exploring the potential of artificial intelligence for restructuring the food packaging sector | Journal of Future Foods (Elsevier / KeAi) 2025 | Reviews the integration of AI, IoT, and machine learning into smart and biodegradable packaging to monitor food conditions. | Shifts focus from static preservation to dynamic "intelligent packaging" that uses embedded nano-biosensors for real-time shelf-life prediction and transparency.
Row 5 : 4. Artificial intelligence in food system: Innovative approach to predicting food spoilage and inventory forecasting | Journal of Agriculture and Food Research (Elsevier)  2025 | Analyzes AI-driven predictive analytics and dynamic algorithms used to forecast spoilage and optimize supply chain storage conditions. | Combines early spoilage detection with real-time environmental data, proving through case studies that AI adjustments can cut retail food waste by nearly 15%.
Row 6 : 5. Artificial intelligence for food safety: From predictive models to real-world safeguards | Trends in Food Science & Technology (Elsevier) 2025 | Explores the use of AI paired with smart sensors and computer vision for the rapid, non-invasive detection of food fraud and contamination. | Bridges the gap between lab testing and field application by emphasizing edge computing and low-cost AI devices for rural and decentralized supply chains.
